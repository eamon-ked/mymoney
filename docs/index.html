Quick Help

Common answers for getting started and preparing for release.

  • Supabase setup: add SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_ANON_KEY in your app’s Info.plist.
  • Sign-in issues: ensure your device has internet and your anon key is valid with proper RLS.
  • Delete Account: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This signs out and clears local data. Server deletion requires the deployed edge function.

FAQ

How are savings and credit cards calculated?
Savings = balances in Checking, Savings, Cash, and Investment. Credit Cards count as debt and subtract from Net Worth.

What counts as monthly expenses?
Expense transactions this month plus any planned loan payments/goal savings you choose to include.

How do I delete my data?
Use Delete Account in Settings. Local data is wiped; server deletion is handled by the edge function when deployed.
